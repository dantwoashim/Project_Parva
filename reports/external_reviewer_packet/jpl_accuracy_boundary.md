# JPL Accuracy Boundary

## What JPL Can Improve

- Solar ingress sensitivity.
- Lunar longitude sensitivity.
- Tithi, nakshatra, yoga, and karana boundary analysis.
- Panchanga at sunrise sensitivity.
- Festival boundary risk analysis.
- Future-BS astronomy candidate evaluation.

## What JPL Cannot Decide

- Official Panchanga authority.
- Civil calendar committee decisions.
- MoHA holiday decisions.
- Institution-specific fiscal or working-day rules.
- Published source authority.

## Current State

The repo now contains an ephemeris kernel policy, kernel hash verifier, JPL adapter boundary, astronomy confidence helpers, and ephemeris accuracy reports. JPL kernels are optional and private/research. Public verification does not require them.

The current report is a foundation and boundary report, not a claim of official civil date accuracy.
