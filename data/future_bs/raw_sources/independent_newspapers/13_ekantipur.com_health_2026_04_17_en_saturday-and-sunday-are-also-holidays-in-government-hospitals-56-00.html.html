The government's decision to grant two days of leave amid the fuel crisis will also be applicable to government hospitals. Health Ministry spokesperson Dr. Prakash Budhathoki has issued a press release, informing that private and emergency services and scheduled surgeries will remain the same. 

'...In accordance with the government's policy of 'two public holidays a week', pre-scheduled surgeries on Sundays, including those scheduled, will be rescheduled to ensure the convenience of patients,' the release said. 

The government's decision to grant two days of leave has worried patients and the hospitals will provide as much services as possible so that patients do not have to suffer during the holidays, Dr. Budhathoki has also stated. The ministry has also requested that arrangements be made to easily refer patients to the nearest appropriate hospital for services that are not available and to keep ambulances on standby at all times. 

Doctors and consumer rights activists have urged the government to reconsider the decision to grant two days off in hospitals as it will further inconvenience patients.
