# Project Parva Licensing

Project Parva is offered through two licensing paths.

## AGPL-3.0-or-later

The public repository is available under `AGPL-3.0-or-later` with no license
fee. The license permits commercial use, modification, distribution, and
self-hosting. Its source-sharing conditions apply to covered works, including
the network-source offer required by AGPL section 13 for modified programs used
over a network.

The full license text in `LICENSE` controls this path. The GNU Project also
publishes the [AGPL-3.0 text](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.en.html) and
[GNU license FAQ](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html).

## Commercial Embedding

A commercial agreement is available for vendors that need to embed named
Project Parva components in proprietary products without applying the AGPL to
the licensed integration. Commercial rights apply only to the components,
products, deployment modes, and legal entity named in the signed agreement.

Annual price per vendor:

| Scope | Annual price | Included product scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Single Product | NPR 100,000 | One named proprietary product |
| Product Suite | NPR 200,000 | Up to three named proprietary products |
| OEM Portfolio | NPR 300,000 | Up to five named products, with redistribution scope written into the agreement |

Taxes, managed hosting, uptime commitments, custom engineering, data-review
work, and support response times are priced separately. Hosted API plans are
also separate from source-code embedding rights.
