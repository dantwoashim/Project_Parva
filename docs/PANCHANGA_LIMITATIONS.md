# Panchanga Limitations

Project Parva Panchanga outputs are computed decision-support artifacts.

They are:

- method-docketed
- ephemeris-dependent
- location/timezone-sensitive
- ayanamsa-sensitive
- review-aware
- replayable where proof fixtures or configured kernels are available

They are not:

- official Panchanga authority
- religious or ritual final authority
- government authority
- legal, tax, payroll, or banking authority

For ritual decisions, users should consult their chosen traditional Panchanga
authority or qualified practitioner. Parva can help explain the computational
inputs, provenance, and boundaries, but it must not replace that authority.
