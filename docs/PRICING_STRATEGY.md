# Pricing Strategy

This is a proposed commercial packaging strategy, not evidence of paying
customers, pilots, adoption, or certification.

| Offer | Scope | Price guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free mini-audit | Up to 50 rows, public-safe report | Free |
| Paid audit | Vendor CSV review and written report | NPR 50,000-150,000 |
| Vendor monthly | Continuous conformance checks | NPR 25,000/month or USD 200/month |
| Enterprise | Private deployment, support, and source policy setup | NPR 1.2M+/year |

The first wedge is BS Date Risk Audit plus Conformance Suite for Nepali
software vendors, payroll, ERP, accounting, cooperatives, microfinance, HR, and
fintech platforms.

Do not present this as customer proof. It is a proposed go-to-market structure.
