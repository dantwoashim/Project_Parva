# Pricing Strategy

Primary wedge: BS Date Risk Audit and Conformance Suite for Nepali software
vendors, ERP, accounting, cooperative, microfinance, HR, payroll software, and
fintech platforms.

| Offer | Buyer | Price guidance |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Mini-audit | technical evaluator | Free for 50 rows |
| Paid audit | vendor team | NPR 50,000-150,000 |
| Vendor monthly | software platform | NPR 25,000/month or USD 200/month |
| Enterprise deployment | regulated or large organization | NPR 1.2M+/year |

Do not lead with banks. Start with vendors that already own date-risk bugs,
support burden, and customer integrations.
