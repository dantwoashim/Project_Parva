# Consumer Panchanga And Kundali Boundary

Status: public-preview boundary.

Panchanga, tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, muhurta, kundali, and personal
calendar surfaces are public-preview or developer-preview decision-support
surfaces. They are computational outputs with method and confidence metadata.
They are not legal, religious, ritual, medical, financial, or government final
authority.

## Public Rules

- Keep method, confidence, source, warnings, and claim-boundary metadata visible.
- Keep expensive computation bounded by route limits or explicit operator
  profiles.
- Treat exact unverified future-date behavior as guarded output.
- Do not describe consumer panchanga, kundali, or muhurta outputs as official or
  final.

## Exposure

Public reference profiles may expose bounded panchanga and muhurta helpers.
Kundali and personal-calendar routes stay in developer or controlled profiles
until their compute, privacy, and documentation posture is stronger.
