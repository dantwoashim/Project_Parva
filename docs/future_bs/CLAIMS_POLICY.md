# Future BS Claims Policy

Parva future BS month lengths are computed predictions, not official publication.

## Allowed Claims

- Parva provides independent computational predictions for unpublished future BS month lengths.
- Parva calibrates and backtests predictions against source-labeled historical data.
- Parva separates high-confidence months from risky months.
- Parva exposes risk labels and aggregate validation posture in public contexts.
- Parva can support controlled comparison of external future BS month-length assumptions in private deployments.
- Parva can support schedule-impact screening in private deployments.

## Disallowed Claims

- Parva guarantees future calendar authority.
- Parva replaces Panchanga Nirnayak Samiti or any official publication authority.
- Parva has solved future calendar publication.
- Scraped or third-party future rows are official evidence.
- Green test results on weak reference data prove official accuracy.

## Required Publication Status

Every unpublished future prediction must carry:

```text
computed_prediction_not_official
```

Known/published historical calendar data may be served from verified corpus or lookup tables. Unpublished future years must stay prediction-scoped and claim-gated.
