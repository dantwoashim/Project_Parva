# Future BS Source Policy

Parva does not treat every calendar row as equal.

Calendar evidence is classified by source strength:

| Tier | Meaning | Claim use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official verified | Government or official publication evidence | Can support official-grade validation |
| Printed verified | Identifiable printed calendar or panchanga evidence | Can support validation after review |
| Public witness | Public dated material linking AD and BS dates | Useful corroboration and review targeting |
| Publisher reference | Public calendar or publisher material | Training and comparison only unless promoted |
| Software/table reference | Open-source or static lookup references | Shadow signal only |
| Third-party reference | App or site references used for comparison | Shadow signal only |
| Needs review | Ambiguous, conflicting, or incomplete evidence | Not claimable |

Only official verified evidence and carefully reviewed printed evidence can support official-grade claims.

Weak, third-party, software-table, or market-shadow sources may help identify disagreement clusters and review targets. They do not become official truth and do not drive public claim-readiness.

All future-BS outputs must preserve the claim boundary:

```text
computed_prediction_not_official
```
