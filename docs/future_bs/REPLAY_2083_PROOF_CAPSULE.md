# 2083 Proof Capsule

This capsule records a narrow research result for public-safe discussion.

## Validation Window

The current official-verified validation window available to Parva covers 2078 to 2083 BS.

In that window:

- the reference-calibrated solar-civil computational path matched 72 out of 72 official month lengths
- the legacy/static baseline matched 68 out of 72 official month lengths

The legacy/static baseline misses were concentrated around boundary-sensitive months:

- 2082 Ashadh
- 2082 Shrawan
- 2083 Bhadra
- 2083 Ashwin

## Interpretation

This result supports continued research into source-aware solar-civil computation and risk labeling.

It is a limited validation result. It is not official authority. It is not a guarantee of future accuracy. It is not a broad future-calendar certainty claim.
