# Future BS Research Layer

The future-BS layer is an experimental risk research system. It studies how month-length assumptions behave before they affect fiscal reports, contracts, renewals, interest periods, exports, and audit trails.

Public documentation describes the methodology and claim boundary. Private deployments can run controlled validation, source-aware comparison, and operational risk review workflows.

The public deployment exposes only:

- future-BS capabilities summary
- source policy summary
- claim boundary
- risk-label vocabulary
- aggregate validation posture

The public deployment does not expose direct future month-length prediction, full-range exports, model runs, backtests, residual analysis, client sheet comparison, or schedule-impact simulation by default.

All future outputs are labeled:

```text
computed_prediction_not_official
```

Current research findings should be read narrowly. A strong validation result in a specific source policy or official window supports the research direction, but it is not broad official future-calendar authority.
