# Committee as Model

Parva does not claim to know or replace official calendar publication choices.

The model-risk engine treats future civil calendar publication as a modeled decision layer:

- astronomical solar ingress computation,
- civil assignment rule candidates,
- month-specific and era-specific precedent,
- source-labeled historical calibration,
- model disagreement,
- perturbation robustness,
- prediction sets and abstention.

The output is a risk-managed decision support layer for financial systems, not official publication.
