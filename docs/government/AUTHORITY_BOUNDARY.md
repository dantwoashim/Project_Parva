# Authority Boundary

Official bodies decide. Parva helps encode, verify, distribute, and test.

Parva must not be described as:

- a replacement for Panchanga,
- a replacement for MoHA, NPNS, or any government body,
- a legal authority,
- a tax authority,
- a banking authority,
- a payroll authority,
- a religious authority,
- an official future-date publisher.

The pilot should preserve existing institutional authority by making source
evidence and release provenance easier to verify.
